# Plain plans and contracts

Plain words, short sentences, exact numbers and dates. No hype.

## A plan starts with
- **Purpose**: the problem and the outcome, in two sentences.
- **Scope**: in and out.
- **Status**: where it stands today.

## Each feature uses these headings
Purpose · Users · Normal process · Rules · Exceptions · Records · Permissions · Completion checks · Open decisions

## Label every item
**Approved** (decided), **Proposed** (suggested, not decided), **Needs decision** (who decides, by when), **Future phase**.

## Contracts answer five questions as headings
1. What you must do
2. What you pay or earn (with a worked example)
3. When you get paid
4. If something goes wrong
5. Suspension, appeals and leaving

Write "you" and "we". Numbers come from the system, not typed by hand. A changed contract is a new version people accept again. A lawyer reviews before launch.

## App text
Screens get short, simple text. Only legal pages, contracts and plans may be long.
